Connect by
Connects through TVT’s cloud relay (c2020.autonat.com): the serial number and the login pass through TVT’s servers. P2P must be switched on in the NVR’s network settings. Video is slower than on the local network, so the full-size view stays on the sub-stream.

Find NVRs on the network

Looks for TVT NVRs two ways: TVT’s own network search (UDP port 23456) and a check of every address below for an NVR answering on its SDK port. Nothing is logged in to or changed.

Networks (192.168.0.0/24, as large as /22), ranges (192.168.2.10-200) or single addresses, up to 1024 in all.

    Sub-streams

    Live grids play each camera’s sub-stream. Browsers on PCs without H.265 hardware decoding can only play H.264, so H.265 sub-streams stay black there. This switches the chosen cameras’ sub-stream to H.264 on the camera itself; main streams and recordings are not changed. You can undo it here.
